About us

Lippe Studios is an independent game studio, founded in November 2026 and based in Tampa, Florida (USA). We make games for phones and PC, always with the same idea: games that are easy to pick up and even better with friends.

What we make

Every game starts with a simple question: what would make someone call a friend to play together? That is how these were born:

  • Milim — the daily word game, to challenge your friends in online rooms.
  • Sarrafo — an arena where 20 fighters battle to be the last one standing.
  • Crav — action and survival in the palm of your hand.
  • Sordim — a survival RPG to play as a group on PC.

How we work

We are a small studio, close to the people who play our games. We test every version before release, read players' suggestions and update our games often. We want each game to speak the player's language: Milim is already in Portuguese, English and Spanish, and this website is in 14 languages.

Partners

We also support Fazay Games, our partner studio, the team behind OVERSPRAY and Poo Tycoon: Total Chaos.
